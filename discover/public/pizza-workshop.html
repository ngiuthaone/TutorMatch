About this workshop

Learn the craft behind a balanced artisan pizza.

Step into a Pizza 4P’s kitchen and learn how dough, sauce, cheese, toppings, and heat come together in a balanced pizza.

Stretch your own dough, choose toppings, build one full pizza, and enjoy it fresh from the oven with the group.

No previous cooking experience is required.

Workshop plan

From dough to your first finished pizza.

Hover a step to preview the moment in the kitchen. The flow stays hands-on, beginner-friendly, and paced so every participant finishes one full pizza.

2.5 hours
01

Welcome and ingredient introduction

Meet the Pizza 4P’s workshop team, get comfortable in the kitchen, and learn how the dough, sauces, cheeses, and seasonal toppings will be used throughout the session.

15 min

02

Dough-stretching demonstration

Watch a chef demonstrate how to open and stretch fermented dough by hand, keep the crust airy, and prepare the base for sauce and toppings without overworking it.

25 min

03

Build your own pizza

Stretch your dough, spread sauce, choose cheeses, and balance toppings with one-on-one guidance from the chefs so your pizza bakes evenly and tastes balanced.

60 min

04

Bake, taste, and wrap up

See how the high-heat oven finishes your pizza, enjoy the tasting together, and leave with a recipe card plus practical tips for recreating the process at home.
