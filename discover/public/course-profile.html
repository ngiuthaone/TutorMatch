About this course

IELTS speaking improves when you know what to do next—not when you memorize a perfect script. This practical course gives you a clear sequence of demonstrations, activities, and review checkpoints.

Linh Nguyen guides each topic with exam-style examples, then gives you focused practice so the method becomes automatic. You can move at your own pace and revisit every lesson with lifetime access.
