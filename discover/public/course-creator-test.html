Film & edit

Use these production guidelines while recording and editing your course videos.

You’re ready to share your knowledge.

Record with the same setup you tested, keep your delivery clear and direct, and edit each lecture so the visuals and audio support the learning objective.

Tips

These pages on Udemy are guidance-only. Your actual videos are added from Curriculum, individually or with the bulk uploader.

01

Script or outline each lecture

Plan what you will say and show before recording. A clear script or speaking outline keeps delivery concise and reduces editing time.

02

Keep one objective per lecture

Make the purpose clear at the start, teach the concept or task, then recap the key takeaway before moving on.

03

Prioritize clear audio

Record close to the microphone, reduce echo and background noise, and make sure audio is present in both channels and synchronized with video.

04

Use dynamic, relevant visuals

Show the instructor, demonstrations, screencasts, diagrams, or other visuals that directly support the narration. Avoid audio-only video or long static slides.

05

Edit distractions out

Remove long pauses, verbal mistakes, repeated takes, and anything that prevents the learner from following the explanation.

06

Export in a learner-ready format

Use horizontal 16:9 video at 720p or 1080p. Check readability at normal playback size before uploading.

07

Keep your original files

Store recordings, project files, slides, and final exports outside the platform so you can revise or replace lectures later.

Technical requirements

HD, horizontal videoMinimum 720p resolution with a 16:9 aspect ratio.
Clear, synchronized audioSpeech must be easy to understand, free of distracting noise, and audible through both channels.
Relevant visual instructionVisuals should be dynamic, custom, and clearly connected to what is being taught.
Concise deliveryEdit out mistakes, long pauses, tangents, and other distractions.
